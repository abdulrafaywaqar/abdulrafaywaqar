## 1. ASSUMPTIONS vs CONSTRAINTS 🎯

### The Key Difference:
- **ASSUMPTIONS** = Things we believe to be true (but might not be)
- **CONSTRAINTS** = Limitations imposed on the project

### Critical Understanding:
- **False Assumptions** = BAD for project ❌
- **False Constraints** = GOOD for project ✅

### Why False Constraints Help:
Constraints are LIMITATIONS. If a constraint turns out to be false, you're removing a limitation!

**Example:**
- **Constraint:** "Budget cannot exceed $100,000"
- **If False:** You can now spend more = POSITIVE impact!
- **Constraint:** "Must finish by December 31st"
- **If False:** You have more time = POSITIVE impact!

### Memory Trick:
**"Constraints CONSTRAIN you - when they're gone, you're FREE!"**

## 2. LEADERSHIP STYLES 👨‍💼

### The Big 6 Leadership Styles:

#### 1. **Autocratic/Authoritarian**
- **"My way or the highway"**
- Leader makes all decisions
- Top-down communication
- Good for: Crisis situations, inexperienced teams

#### 2. **Democratic/Participative**
- **"Let's decide together"**
- Team input in decisions
- Final decision with leader
- Good for: Experienced teams, complex decisions

#### 3. **Laissez-faire**
- **"You handle it"**
- Hands-off approach
- Team makes decisions
- Good for: Highly skilled, self-motivated teams

#### 4. **Servant Leadership**
- **"How can I help you succeed?"**
- Leader serves the team
- Removes obstacles
- Good for: Agile environments, empowered teams

#### 5. **Transactional**
- **"Do this, get that"**
- Reward and punishment system
- Clear expectations
- Good for: Routine work, clear processes

#### 6. **Transformational**
- **"Let's change the world together"**
- Inspires and motivates
- Creates vision
- Good for: Change initiatives, innovation

### Memory Tricks:
- **Autocratic** = "Auto" = Automatic decisions (no input)
- **Democratic** = Like voting in democracy
- **Laissez-faire** = French for "let it be"
- **Servant** = Leader serves others
- **Transactional** = Transaction (give and take)
- **Transformational** = Transform/change

## 3. CONTRACT TYPES 📝

### When to Use Each Contract Type:

#### **Fixed Price (FP)**
- **When:** Scope is clear and well-defined
- **Risk:** Seller bears the risk
- **Example:** Building a standard house with known specs
- **Buyer Risk:** LOW ✅

#### **Time & Materials (T&M)**
- **When:** Scope is unclear or evolving
- **Risk:** Shared between buyer and seller
- **Example:** Software development with changing requirements
- **Buyer Risk:** MEDIUM

#### **Cost Plus (CPIF/CPFF)**
- **When:** High uncertainty, R&D projects
- **Risk:** Buyer bears the risk
- **Example:** Developing new technology
- **Buyer Risk:** HIGH ⚠️

### Contract Memory Formula:
**Uncertainty Level = Risk Level for Buyer**
- High Uncertainty → Cost Plus (High Buyer Risk)
- Medium Uncertainty → T&M (Medium Buyer Risk)  
- Low Uncertainty → Fixed Price (Low Buyer Risk)

## 4. EARNED VALUE MANAGEMENT (EVM) 📊

### The Big 3 Values:
- **PV (Planned Value)** = What you planned to accomplish
- **EV (Earned Value)** = What you actually accomplished
- **AC (Actual Cost)** = What you actually spent

### Key Formulas:

#### Performance Indices:
- **CPI = EV / AC** (Cost Performance)
- **SPI = EV / PV** (Schedule Performance)

#### Interpretations:
- **CPI > 1** = Under budget ✅
- **CPI < 1** = Over budget ❌
- **SPI > 1** = Ahead of schedule ✅
- **SPI < 1** = Behind schedule ❌

#### Forecasting:
- **EAC = BAC / CPI** (when current performance continues)
- **ETC = EAC - AC** (Estimate to Complete)

### Memory Trick:
**"CPI = Cost Performance, SPI = Schedule Performance"**
**"Greater than 1 = Great! Less than 1 = Less than ideal!"**

## 5. AGILE TRIANGLE ▲

### Traditional Triangle:
- **Fixed:** Scope
- **Variable:** Time & Cost

### Agile Triangle (INVERTED):
- **Fixed:** Time & Cost (iterations/sprints)
- **Variable:** Scope

### Why Inverted?
In Agile, you work in fixed timeboxes (sprints) with fixed teams (cost), but scope can change based on priorities and feedback.

### Memory Trick:
**"Agile is flexible with SCOPE, rigid with TIME and COST"**

## 6. AGILE CEREMONIES 🏃‍♂️

### Sprint Planning:
- **Who:** Product Owner, Scrum Master, Development Team
- **When:** Start of sprint
- **What:** Plan work for upcoming sprint

### Sprint Review:
- **Who:** Everyone + Stakeholders
- **When:** End of sprint
- **What:** Demo completed work, get feedback
- **Product-focused:** YES ✅

### Sprint Retrospective:
- **Who:** Scrum Team only
- **When:** After Sprint Review
- **What:** Improve process
- **Process-focused:** YES ✅

### Planning Poker:
1. Read user story
2. Team discusses and asks questions
3. Everyone picks estimation card privately
4. Reveal cards simultaneously
5. **If different:** Discuss reasons (high/low estimators explain)
6. Re-estimate after discussion
7. Repeat until consensus

### Memory Trick:
**"Review = Show product, Retrospective = Improve process"**

## 7. ORGANIZATIONAL FACTORS 🏢

### Enterprise Environmental Factors (EEFs):
- **What you CANNOT control**
- Organizational culture
- Government regulations
- Market conditions
- Governance frameworks

### Organizational Process Assets (OPAs):
- **What the organization HAS learned**
- Templates
- Lessons learned
- Corporate knowledge base
- Historical information

### Memory Trick:
**"EEFs = External/Environmental (can't control)"**
**"OPAs = Organization's Past Assets (can use)"**

## Practice Questions 💪

### Question 1: Leadership
*A PM gives team members complete freedom to make decisions and solve problems. This is:*
a) Servant leadership
b) Laissez-faire
c) Democratic
d) Transformational

**Answer: B** - Laissez-faire = hands-off, complete freedom

### Question 2: Constraints
*When a time constraint turns out to be false, it affects the project:*
a) Negatively
b) Positively
c) Depends on the constraint
d) No impact

**Answer: B** - False constraint = removed limitation = positive!

### Question 3: Contracts
*You need technical experts for unclear scope work. Best contract:*
a) Fixed Price
b) Cost Plus
c) Time & Materials
d) CPIF

**Answer: C** - T&M for unclear scope + expert resources
