## 🎯 LESSON 1: ASSUMPTIONS vs CONSTRAINTS - The Foundation

### Let's Start with a Story:

**Meet Sarah, the Project Manager:**
Sarah is planning a wedding (yes, it's a project!). She makes this list:

**Sarah's List:**
1. "The venue must be booked by March 15th" 
2. "I think Uncle Bob will DJ for free"
3. "Budget cannot exceed $50,000"
4. "The weather will probably be nice in June"
5. "All vendors must be local"

**Now, let's categorize:**

**CONSTRAINTS (Rules/Limitations):**
- "Venue must be booked by March 15th" ⏰
- "Budget cannot exceed $50,000" 💰
- "All vendors must be local" 📍

**ASSUMPTIONS (Beliefs/Hopes):**
- "Uncle Bob will DJ for free" 🎵
- "Weather will be nice in June" ☀️

### The Magic Question: "What if this is FALSE?"

**If Constraint is False:**
- "Actually, venue can be booked until April 1st" → MORE TIME = GOOD! ✅
- "Budget can go up to $60,000" → MORE MONEY = GOOD! ✅
- "We can use out-of-town vendors" → MORE OPTIONS = GOOD! ✅

**If Assumption is False:**
- "Uncle Bob wants $500 to DJ" → UNEXPECTED COST = BAD! ❌
- "June weather is terrible" → BACKUP PLANS NEEDED = BAD! ❌

### The Universal Rule:
**False Constraints = Removing Handcuffs = FREEDOM = POSITIVE**
**False Assumptions = Reality Check = PROBLEMS = NEGATIVE**

- **When she shines:** Complex decisions, experienced team
- **Example:** City council meeting with expert advisors

- **Example:** University professors doing research

- **Motto:** "How can I help you succeed?"

- **Example:** Agile coach removing obstacles

- **Motto:** "Do this, get that"
- **When he shines:** Clear processes, routine work
- **Example:** Sales manager with commission structure
- **Your signal:** Clear metrics, routine tasks, motivation needed

- **Motto:** "Together we'll change the world!"

## 📝 LESSON 3: CONTRACT TYPES - The Risk-O-Meter

### The Contract Risk Scale:

Think of contracts like insurance policies. Who bears the risk?

```
LOW BUYER RISK ←―――――――――――――――――――――――→ HIGH BUYER RISK
     FP              T&M              CPFF
(Seller Risk)    (Shared Risk)    (Buyer Risk)
```

### The Three-Story Building Analogy:

**GROUND FLOOR - FIXED PRICE (FP):** 🏠
- **When:** Building a McDonald's restaurant (standard design)
- **Why:** Everything is known, specifications are clear
- **Risk:** Seller takes all the risk
- **Example:** "Build this exact house for $200,000, no matter what"

**MIDDLE FLOOR - TIME & MATERIALS (T&M):** 🔧
- **When:** Renovating an old house (surprises expected)
- **Why:** Scope might change, need flexibility
- **Risk:** Shared between buyer and seller
- **Example:** "Pay me $50/hour + materials, we'll see what we find"

**TOP FLOOR - COST PLUS (CP):** 🔬
- **When:** Inventing a time machine (no one knows how)
- **Why:** Pure research, high uncertainty
- **Risk:** Buyer takes all the risk
- **Example:** "Pay all my costs + fee, no promises on final price"

### The Uncertainty Rule:
**More Uncertainty = More Risk for Buyer**

### Contract Selector Tool:

**Question 1:** How clear is the scope?
- Very clear → FP
- Somewhat clear → T&M  
- Unclear → CP

**Question 2:** How much can requirements change?
- Won't change → FP
- Might change → T&M
- Will definitely change → CP

**Question 3:** How much risk can you handle?
- Minimize risk → FP
- Share risk → T&M
- Accept high risk → CP

## 📊 LESSON 4: EARNED VALUE MANAGEMENT - The Project Health Check

### The Medical Analogy:

Think of your project as a patient, and EVM as the vital signs:

**The Three Vital Signs:**
- **PV (Planned Value)** = Scheduled checkup time 📅
- **EV (Earned Value)** = What actually got accomplished 💪
- **AC (Actual Cost)** = How much money was spent 💰

### The Two Health Indicators:

**CPI (Cost Performance Index) = EV ÷ AC**
- **CPI > 1.0** = "Patient is financially healthy!" 💚
- **CPI < 1.0** = "Patient is overspending!" 🔴
- **CPI = 1.0** = "Patient is on budget!" 🟡

**SPI (Schedule Performance Index) = EV ÷ PV**
- **SPI > 1.0** = "Patient is ahead of schedule!" 💚
- **SPI < 1.0** = "Patient is behind schedule!" 🔴
- **SPI = 1.0** = "Patient is on time!" 🟡

### Doctor's Diagnosis Examples:

**Patient A:** CPI = 1.2, SPI = 0.8
- **Diagnosis:** "Under budget but behind schedule"
- **Treatment:** "Add resources to catch up"

**Patient B:** CPI = 0.9, SPI = 1.1  
- **Diagnosis:** "Over budget but ahead of schedule"
- **Treatment:** "Review costs, maybe slow down"

**Patient C:** CPI = 1.0, SPI = 1.0
- **Diagnosis:** "Perfect health!"
- **Treatment:** "Keep doing what you're doing"

### Practice Problems:

**Project Healthy:**
- BAC = $100,000
- PV = $60,000 (should have done)
- EV = $70,000 (actually completed)
- AC = $65,000 (actually spent)

**Calculate:**
1. CPI = $70,000 ÷ $65,000 = _______
2. SPI = $70,000 ÷ $60,000 = _______
3. Diagnosis: _______ budget, _______ schedule

## ▲ LESSON 5: AGILE TRIANGLE - The Flexibility Framework

### Traditional vs Agile - The Restaurant Analogy:

**TRADITIONAL RESTAURANT (Waterfall):**
- **Fixed:** Menu items (scope)
- **Variable:** Cooking time and cost
- **Problem:** "Sorry, we're out of salmon, come back next week"

**AGILE FOOD TRUCK (Agile):**
- **Fixed:** Operating hours and staff (time & cost)
- **Variable:** Menu items (scope)
- **Solution:** "No salmon? How about fish tacos instead?"

### The Agile Promise:

**Traditional Promise:** "We'll deliver exactly this, but it might take longer and cost more"

**Agile Promise:** "We'll work for exactly this time with this team, and deliver the most valuable features possible"

### Why It Works:

**Scenario:** Halfway through your project, the market changes.

**Traditional Response:**
- "We can't change now, we planned everything"
- Project delivers something nobody wants

**Agile Response:**
- "Let's pivot and deliver what's now most valuable"
- Project delivers exactly what's needed

### The Flexibility Formula:
**Fixed Resources + Fixed Time = Maximum Value**

## 🏃‍♂️ LESSON 6: AGILE CEREMONIES - The Team Rituals

### The Sprint Journey:

Think of a sprint as a mini-project with its own lifecycle:

**SPRINT PLANNING** 📋 (Start of journey)
- **Who:** Product Owner + Scrum Team
- **What:** "What will we build this sprint?"
- **Analogy:** Planning a 2-week road trip

**DAILY STANDUP** ☕ (Daily check-in)
- **Who:** Development Team
- **What:** "Where are we today?"
- **Analogy:** Daily family dinner updates

**SPRINT REVIEW** 🎬 (Show and tell)
- **Who:** Everyone + Stakeholders  
- **What:** "Look what we built!"
- **Focus:** PRODUCT demonstration
- **Analogy:** Movie premiere screening

**SPRINT RETROSPECTIVE** 🔄 (Lessons learned)
- **Who:** Scrum Team only
- **What:** "How can we improve?"
- **Focus:** PROCESS improvement
- **Analogy:** Family meeting after vacation

### The Memory Trick:
**"Review = Show PRODUCT, Retrospective = Improve PROCESS"**

### Planning Poker - The Estimation Game:

**Step-by-Step:**
1. **Product Owner:** "Here's the user story..."
2. **Team:** Asks questions and discusses
3. **Everyone:** Picks estimation card secretly 🃏
4. **Everyone:** Reveals cards at same time
5. **If different:** High and low estimators explain why
6. **Team:** Discusses different perspectives
7. **Repeat:** Until everyone agrees

**Key Rule:** DISCUSS before re-estimating!

## 🏢 LESSON 7: EEFs vs OPAs - What You Control vs What Controls You

### The House Analogy:

**EEFs (Enterprise Environmental Factors) = The Neighborhood**
- Weather (can't control) 🌦️
- Local laws (must follow) 📜
- Crime rate (external factor) 🚨
- Economic conditions (market forces) 📈

**OPAs (Organizational Process Assets) = Your House Toolkit**
- Tools in your garage 🔧
- Family recipes 📖
- Photo albums (memories) 📸
- Instruction manuals 📋

### The Control Test:

**Ask yourself:** "Can I change this?"

**If NO:** It's probably an EEF
- Government regulations
- Industry standards  
- Organizational culture
- Market conditions

**If YES (or can use it):** It's probably an OPA
- Templates
- Lessons learned
- Procedures
- Historical data

### Quick Sorting Exercise:

1. "Company's project management methodology"
   - **Can you use it?** Yes → OPA ✅

2. "New government tax law"
   - **Can you control it?** No → EEF ✅

3. "Lessons learned from previous projects"
   - **Can you use it?** Yes → OPA ✅

4. "Industry safety standards"
   - **Can you control it?** No → EEF ✅
