## 🎯 ASSUMPTIONS vs CONSTRAINTS DRILL

### Scenario Practice:
For each situation, identify if it's an ASSUMPTION or CONSTRAINT, and determine the impact if it turns out to be FALSE:

**Situation 1:** "The client must approve all deliverables within 2 business days"
- **Type:** Constraint (limitation on approval time)
- **If False:** More time for approvals = POSITIVE impact

**Situation 2:** "The team assumes the new software will integrate easily with existing systems"
- **Type:** Assumption (belief about integration)
- **If False:** Integration problems = NEGATIVE impact

**Your Turn - Practice Problems:**
